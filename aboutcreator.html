About me

Hi, I'm Rwechungura Evodius Lutta. Friends call me Twarvis Davinc. I am studying an Ordinary Diploma in Communication System Technology at the Dar es Salaam Institute of Technology (DIT), where I learn how networks and communication systems carry information from one place to another.

I am also a member of CyberClub DIT. There I practise cybersecurity through hands-on labs, Linux work and Capture the Flag (CTF) challenges, and I learn how systems are attacked so that I can understand how to defend them.

Beyond my studies I design and build websites. My portfolio of work lives at twarvis.cc.cd.
