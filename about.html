About Student Hub

Everything you need to know about the platform, the creator, my other projects, and free Creative Commons tools we love.

What is Student Hub?

Student Hub is a free, student-built platform made to help learners share, discover, and organize study materials — all in one calm, distraction-free space. It was born from a simple idea: someone already took this note — so why not share it?

Whether you're revising for exams, catching up on a missed lecture, or looking for past papers, Student Hub connects you with what other students have already uploaded.

  • Upload notes & papers — share PDFs, slides, docs, images and archives in seconds.
  • See uploaded notes — browse everything other students have shared.
  • Smart search — find files fast by module, course code, or keyword.
  • Built-in scanner — photograph handwritten pages and turn them into clean PDFs.
  • Private mode — protect sensitive files with a 4-digit passcode.
  • Works everywhere — fully responsive on phone, tablet, and desktop.
